# P7-08 — Transition to Business as Usual

**Wave 4. Depends on P7-05, P7-07.**

**Phase 7 has no cutover, no parallel run and no rollback — nothing goes live.** Its operational item is
the one nobody schedules: **the programme ends, and everything it built has to be owned by someone
afterwards.**

## What has no permanent owner by default

Seven years of delivery leaves a set of standing obligations that a project team has been absorbing
informally. **Each has been flagged somewhere in the corpus as needing an owner, and each is invisible
once the team disperses:**

| Obligation | Flagged in | What happens without an owner |
|---|---|---|
| **The model inventory** | `D15` q2 | *"An inventory nobody maintains is worse than none, because it reports coverage that has decayed"* |
| **The periodic revalidation cycle** | `D15-2` | Models silently pass their due dates |
| **The outstanding-items report** | `d15-control-core` §6.2 | *"Becomes a page nobody opens"* |
| **Break, suspense and IPV review forums** | P0-16, P2-16 | Controls that operate only while someone convenes them |
| **Rule authorship custody** — accounting, regulatory, factor sets | P0-15, P1-01 | Interim owners were named for Phase 0/1; **D7 and D13 take custody, and the handover is not automatic** |
| **The interim non-rate representation owner** | `D11-10`, P1-15 | Hands to D11, which by now exists |
| **The FTP methodology** | P6-01 | Finance owns integrity, treasury operates — confirm it held |
| **Scenario calibration vintages** | D14 §4 | *"Scenarios expire"* — and a stale one reports "survived" until it does not |
| **Approaching clocks** — proxy retirement, look-back coverage, licence renewals | P1-09, P2-02 | Deadlines with no diary |

## The interim owners were always temporary

**Three interim arrangements were created deliberately, on the reasoning that a later module would take
custody of something already in production** — a better position than authoring from scratch. That
reasoning only holds **if the handover actually happens.**

**This ticket is where the temporary arrangements are made permanent or explicitly renewed.**

## In scope

- **A named permanent owner for every obligation above**, with a review cycle
- **Handover of the interim rule authorship arrangements** to their intended custodians, or documented
  renewal
- **The standing forum set**, consolidated — several were created independently and some can merge
- **A deadline register**: proxy retirement, licence renewals, revalidation dates, scenario vintages,
  look-back coverage milestones
- **Documentation handover** — the corpus is the design record and needs a maintainer, or it becomes
  archaeology
- **Programme closure report**: what was built, what was deferred, what remains open

## Out of scope

- Aggregate reporting — P7-03
- The board and regulator pack — P7-07
- Validation resourcing — P7-05, which this confirms rather than decides

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Every obligation in the table has a named permanent owner and a review cycle** — a function, not an
   individual
2. Interim rule authorship has **transferred to its intended custodian or been explicitly renewed** with
   a reason
3. A consolidated forum set exists, with membership, cycle and authority to act
4. **A deadline register exists** and is owned, covering every dated obligation the platform carries
5. The design corpus has a maintainer and a statement of what it is authoritative for
6. **Deferred items are listed with their triggers**, so the next programme does not rediscover them

## Notes

**Criterion 6 is the cheapest thing in this ticket and the most likely to be skipped.** The corpus
already uses deferred-with-trigger discipline for amendments — *"a deferred finding with no ref and no
trigger is a finding that will be found again from scratch by the next agent."* **The same applies to
deferred scope at programme close**: intraday liquidity, the Tier 3 valuation path, IRB, macro hedge
accounting, and everything the group structure answer may open up.

**This ticket is not a formality and its absence has a predictable shape.** Eighteen months after a
programme ends, the inventory is stale, the forums have quietly stopped, the interim owners have moved
on, and nobody can say whether the revalidation cycle is running. **None of that is anyone's fault; it is
the default outcome of not naming owners while the team still exists.**
